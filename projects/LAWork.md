For the Spring and Fall 2025 semesters at University of Hawaii at Manoa, I worked as a Learning Assistant for MATH 134 (Precalculus: Elementary Functions) and MATH 140X (Precalculus) for the respective semesters. Under John C. Robertson, I gained useful skills in learning how to connect with peers and help further their journey within math education.

As a Learning Assistant, I was also required to take EDCS 417, which is a basic pedagogy class to teach learning assistants important concepts within education and to learn how to connect with students in order to be a better educator.
