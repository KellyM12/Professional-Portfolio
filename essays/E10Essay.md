Despite being a senior, I always feel like adjusting to a new term can be challenging, especially during the first few weeks. Perhaps it’s just that sometimes I have difficulty adjusting to new things. During this first week of the Summer Term, I’ve had some ups and downs but I think I have managed to get through it relatively well. I have enjoyed getting back into computer science again. Taking the course ICS 314 – Software Engineering I, I’ve learned a lot about both the updated JavaScript and Typescript, which I thought was interesting.

I really like using Typescript; I think that it’s relatively intuitive to use all things considered. I have heard about it before from a friend of mine who majored in electrical engineering, but I had never actually used it myself. It’s very similar to JavaScript, which I do know how to use and have used for many years. Although some of the syntax is different, and it takes a while for me to get used to, all of the changes seem to make sense. From a software engineering perspective, I think that Typescript is very useful, is easy and intuitive to learn, and handles data structures and error marking well. Thus, this makes it a good programming language to learn for software engineering.

I really liked the practice WODs and enjoyed doing them. I think that they were very useful in terms of communicating expectations of the course. Overall, I really do like this style of learning (athletic software engineering). I do realize that I should try to do more things ahead of time, in case of unexpected occurrences like illness. Other than that, I do think that this course will work out well for me over all. I like the learning style with the course website, although I wish that the readings and lectures were separated into different sections.

Overall, I think managing my time between everything that I’m doing will be the true challenge this Summer Term. Thankfully with good instructors and mentors, I am excited to have software engineering as one of my classes this semester.
